Phototape

Turn a photo into printable journaling tape. Upload → select area → pick palette → print on A4 adhesive paper.

1. Source

Drag on the image to select a region. Click without dragging to select the whole image.

2. Palette

smaller = more, tighter modes · larger = fewer, broader

3. Quantized preview

4. Tape

0 = proportional to pixel counts · 100 = uniform bands
Click a tape band to drop that color (its pixels are discarded, remaining bands resize). Click a swatch to redistribute it (its pixels go to the nearest remaining color). Click again to restore. Drag bands to reorder.
